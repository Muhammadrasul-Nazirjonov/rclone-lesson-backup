Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who uses this app

This app is a private rclone configuration used by one person, its owner, to back up their own files to their own Google Drive. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

The app requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope. With it, the app can create, read, update and delete only the files and folders that the app itself created in the owner's Drive. It cannot see or open any other file.

How the data is used

  • Files are copied from the owner's computer to the owner's Google Drive, and checked against the originals.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is collected by, or sent to, the app's author or any other party. There are no analytics, cookies, advertising or tracking.
  • Google user data is never sold, shared, transferred or used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account under Security → Third-party apps & services.
